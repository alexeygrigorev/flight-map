# Data and network services

Bundled on 2026-10-18 using `tools/update_data.py`:

- OurAirports, public-domain airport database: https://ourairports.com/data/ . Airports with IATA codes and type medium/large; airport time zones inferred from the nearest GeoNames city in the same country. Confirm ticket times, especially near time-zone borders.
- GeoNames cities5000: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/ , CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . This product includes data from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org). Fields reduced to ID, name, country, coordinates, time zone, population.
- Natural Earth 1:110m land: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ , public domain. Source https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson . Geometry reduced by the import script.

Weather: Open-Meteo https://open-meteo.com/ , CC BY 4.0. Coordinates for corridor cities are sent only during preparation. Hourly temperature, WMO weather code and day/night, in Unix UTC timestamps. Forecast horizon approximately 16 days; preparation reports unavailable dates. Cache reused for up to three hours, refreshed on later preparation. Saved forecasts remain available offline but are not live observations.

Map: MapLibre Native Android 13.6.1. OpenFreeMap https://openfreemap.org/ provides vector tiles, © OpenStreetMap contributors https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and OpenMapTiles https://openmaptiles.org/ . Policy https://openfreemap.org/quick_start/ (checked 2026-10-18, re-verified 2026-10-20) explicitly permits offline downloads at most 3,000 tiles or 50 MB per download, four parallel requests and a unique User-Agent ("Offline downloads are allowed up to 3,000 tiles or 50 MB per download, with at most 4 parallel requests and a unique User-Agent"). Each flight pack keeps one aggregate budget across its detail tiers (broad overview z0–6, corridor z7–9 adapted to budget, airports z9–10), capped at 2,900 tiles / 49 MB per download with headroom below those limits. Route geometries split at the date line; detail is concentrated in a narrow route band and around airports. Natural Earth is the permanent offline base. Native offline regions retain corridor resources in private app storage. Android removal/clear-storage deletes them. Note: the cached vector regions are retained offline but are not drawn on the custom 3D globe - the globe renders bundled relief + Natural Earth borders plus the NASA imagery below; at deep zoom the coarse bundled water polygons fade out wherever detailed imagery is shown.

Corridor imagery (deep-zoom globe detail): NASA Visible Earth Blue Marble Next Generation, Shaded Relief and Bathymetry, served as static 256px JPEG tiles through the NASA GIBS WMTS REST endpoint (https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wmts/epsg3857/best/BlueMarble_ShadedRelief_Bathymetry, GoogleMapsCompatible_Level8, levels 0–8, verified live 2026-10-20). GIBS is a programmatic tile API designed for machine tile access - this is NOT bulk-scraping the community-funded tile.openstreetmap.org raster servers, whose Tile Usage Policy (https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/, verified 2026-10-20) explicitly forbids bulk download/prefetch and offline use ("Offline use is not permitted on tile.openstreetmap.org"). NASA media guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/, verified 2026-10-20) state NASA content is generally not subject to copyright in the US and may be used for educational/informational works including computer graphical simulations with NASA acknowledged as source. The app shows "Imagery: NASA Visible Earth" attribution on the globe screen and in About. Preparation downloads only the bounded route corridor + airport boxes at z7–z8 (GIBS maximum for this layer, ~0.6 km/px at the equator, ~16x sharper than the bundled 4096×2048 relief at ~9.4 km/px), capped at 1,200 tiles / 25 MB per flight (measured 2026-10-20: HEL–PVG 289 tiles ≈ 3.3 MB; SIN–EWR 794 tiles ≈ 9.1 MB). Downloads use ≤4 parallel requests, retries with backoff, atomic file writes, skip-valid resume across pause/restart, and verify every tile (JPEG magic + size) before reporting ready. In flight the globe samples these files offline and falls back to bundled relief outside cached coverage; no network is used in flight.

Flight lookup deliberately requires no commercial key: AY87 HEL–PVG is a bundled schedule fallback, 01:10 Helsinki to 18:25 Shanghai, 12h15m. Route and example times were cross-checked against https://aviability.com/flight-number/flight-ay87-finnair and https://www.flight.info/AY87 on 2026-10-18. **These are example schedule data, not a date-verified booking or live flight status.** Confirm against a ticket; all airports, local departure/arrival dates and times are editable. Unknown flight numbers use manual entry. No flight tracking API, GPS, account or secret is used.
